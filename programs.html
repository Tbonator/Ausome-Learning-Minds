Our programs

Learning that helps every child thrive

Our programs combine the CAPS and DCAPS curriculum with a nature-based approach, individual remedial therapy, and dedicated parent support to ensure each child thrives academically, socially, and emotionally.

What we offer

Six ways we support your child's learning journey.

01

CAPS & DCAPS curriculum

Grade RR – 7

Structured learning from Grade RR to 7 following the CAPS & DCAPS curriculum to build strong academic foundations.

02

Individual remedial therapy

Personalized remedial therapy tailored to each child's unique learning needs and pace.

03

Robotics for kids

Our Robotics for Kids program introduces learners to problem-solving, creativity, and teamwork through fun, hands-on technology projects.

04

Nature-based approach

A nature-based approach that encourages hands-on learning and exploration in a stimulating outdoor environment.

05

Parent support

Guidance and resources for parents to actively support their child's learning journey.

06

Extra mural activities

Additional cost

Exciting extra mural activities, including swimming, soccer, and volleyball, are available at an additional cost to encourage fitness, teamwork, and fun.
